## Required evidence matrix

Use official product pages, pricing/checkout terms, help-center articles, subscription/cancellation policies and published clinical/refill documentation. Link the exact page for every substantive claim and record access date. Compare at least two directly relevant brands per confirmed therapy where public evidence exists; use more for major categories. Do not force a match for ambiguous or unavailable products.

For each brand/product record:

- Actual product, ingredients, strength, route, package quantity and stated days of supply. Record prescription versus nonprescription and branded versus compounded status when evidenced.
- Published price, currency, introductory period, renewal price, billing cadence, minimum commitment, upfront amount and cancellation/refund terms. A monthly equivalent on a prepaid plan is not a monthly charge.
- Medication, clinician/service membership, lab, consultation, shipping and supply fees: included, separate or undisclosed.
- Dose/strength model: fixed formulation, clinician-selected dose, starter versus maintenance tier, dose-based price tiers, or one price across doses. Record ingredient strength, concentration, package volume/count and total active amount separately when published. Preserve the source units; do not equate mg, mL, syringe units or number of doses. Do not produce patient dosing instructions.
- Describe publicly documented dose-change workflows: whether a new clinician review/prescription, new SKU/offer, price change, additional charge or shipment adjustment is required. Separate published product options from individualized prescribing decisions. Mark unverified titration/escalation rules as undisclosed.
- Subscription model: monthly recurring, multi-month prepaid, membership plus medication, recurring medication shipment, treatment-course purchase, or per-visit/per-refill purchase. Record service access, billing interval, medicine supply and prescription validity independently.
- First payment timing relative to consultation, prescribing approval, pharmacy release and shipment. Record what happens if prescribing is declined only when explicitly published.
- Refill initiation: automatic shipment, patient request, questionnaire, clinician review or another stated workflow. Distinguish prescription refills from repeat shipping under an existing prescription.
- Published check-in timing, prescription duration, refill limits, dose-change review, labs/monitoring and pause/hold/restart requirements. Mark undisclosed details as **not publicly documented**; do not invent intervals.
- What happens after a payment failure or canceled membership: distinguish loss of service/refill access from medication discontinuation or prescription validity. Report only documented brand behavior.
- Marketing statements versus enforceable terms versus documented clinician protocols. Do not treat an automatic billing date as clinical approval.
- Confidence, unresolved questions and whether a public page, account-only workflow or direct confirmation would be needed to establish the claim.
